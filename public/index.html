About the Restaurant

Annam Brahma is a restaurant serving European, Mediterranean, Japanese and Asian cuisine, which combines the skills of high-class chefs and courteous service.


The calm and elegant interior, decorated in the best traditions of classical design, creates a warm atmosphere that perfectly combines with the culinary delights of our impressions restaurant.

The chef

Today, in our hurried age, we don't have enough energy, time, and, let's say, imagination to cook a delicious homemade dinner. That's why the fast food and convenience food market has flooded our lives. It's time to change that.


Our mission is to make your life easier, free you from shopping trips, give you the opportunity to devote more time to your family and delight yourself and your loved ones with great restaurant-level dishes every day.


By becoming our guest, you not only get aesthetic pleasure from the interior, you share our philosophy of nutritious, delicious, well-cooked food.
